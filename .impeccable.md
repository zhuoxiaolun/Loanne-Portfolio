# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary audience:** Recruiters, design hiring managers, and senior product designers evaluating Loanne for UX/product roles.

**Job to be done:** Within 5 seconds of landing, the visitor should feel: *"This person is rigorous and thoughtful — and she's genuinely curious, always learning."* They're looking for evidence of depth: real research, real decisions, a real perspective — not a pretty portfolio that could belong to anyone.

**Context of use:** Desktop-first (recruiters reviewing portfolios at work), but mobile must feel intentional — not just a shrunk desktop view.

---

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** 好奇心（Curious） · 現代感（Modern） · 充滿人性（Deeply human）

**Voice:** Thoughtful without being precious. Warm without being casual. Bilingual (English brand surface, Traditional Chinese depth) — the language switch itself expresses that Loanne moves between registers.

**Emotional goal:** The portfolio should feel like meeting a person, not browsing a product. It should have a point of view. Small choices — a pull quote, an asymmetric layout, an unexpected transition — should feel authored, not generated.

---

### Aesthetic Direction

**Theme:** Light mode. The existing rose/pink radial background halo is a keeper — it's the warmth underneath the professionalism.

**Visual tone:** Editorial. Not magazine-loud, but the kind of quiet editorial that uses whitespace with intention, breaks the grid for emphasis, and treats typography as hierarchy AND personality simultaneously.

**Reference:** [Kat Wang's portfolio](https://kat-wang.framer.website) — personal character, not template-driven. What resonates: the sense that a real person made deliberate choices.

**Anti-references:**
- Generic AI portfolio templates (icon + heading + 3 bullet points, repeated 6 times)
- Glassmorphism and glow effects
- Cyan-on-dark "tech" palettes
- Any layout that could swap out the name and belong to someone else

**Typography direction:** Keep Cormorant for display — it's already brand-embedded and has genuine character. Push the body pairing harder; Noto Sans TC is functional but could have a more interesting Latin companion.

**Color:** Lake Blue + Cherry core is right. Develop the palette *around* them with more tonal depth — tinted neutrals, not flat grays. The rose background halo is a signature; lean into it.

**Motion:** Expressive and emotional. This is NOT a "subtle 200ms fade" portfolio — it can have staged reveals, scroll-driven storytelling, fluid transitions between pages. Motion should feel like it's *showing* thought, not just animating for decoration.

---

### Design Principles

1. **Curiosity as composition** — Layouts invite exploration. Use asymmetry, varied rhythm, and deliberate grid breaks so the eye has a journey, not just a scan path.

2. **Warmth underneath the precision** — Every rigorous element (data, process, metrics) should sit inside a warm, human container. The rose halo, the Cormorant italics, the Cherry accents — these are the emotion layer.

3. **Authored, not assembled** — Each page should feel like Loanne chose these specific proportions, this specific spacing, this specific moment to break the grid. No component should look like it came from a library untouched.

4. **Motion tells the story** — Transitions and reveals are narrative tools. A project case study that unfolds beat by beat is more persuasive than a static page dump. Use motion to build tension, then release it.

5. **Bilingual as a feature, not a footnote** — The switch between English and Traditional Chinese is itself an expression of Loanne's range. Typography and spacing must handle both scripts with equal care and intention.
